POP SORT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Pop Sort (package com.popsort.game, “the game”) handles, and why.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no chat and no analytics.
  • Your progress, settings and simple play statistics are stored only on your device and are never uploaded by the game.
  • If advertising is switched on in a version of the game, the ad provider (Unity LevelPlay and its partners) handles some data to show ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

To save your game and make it play well, the game keeps the following on your phone only:

  • Progress: levels reached, stars, coins and boosters.
  • Settings: music, sound and vibration on/off.
  • Play statistics: how many times and on how many days you have played, how many levels you have won, and how many times you have failed a level. The game uses these to give a little extra help on a level you keep failing, and to space out ads so they don’t appear too early or too often.

This information never leaves your device through the game, and the developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

The current version does not show ads. When ads are switched on, the game may show:

  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to continue a level or double your coins), and
  • Occasional ads between levels, never during your first levels, and with limits on how often they appear.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks. To show, measure and limit ads, and to prevent fraud, they may collect your device’s advertising ID, general device information (such as model and operating system), your approximate location from your IP address, and information about which ads you see and tap. Depending on your choices and where you live, ads may be personalised.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device’s Settings ▸ Google ▸ Ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Unity’s privacy policy explains how Unity and its partners use this data: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback (you can turn it off in the pause menu). It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and deletion

Data stored on your device stays under your control: uninstalling the game removes it. For data held by the ad provider, see Unity’s privacy policy above, or contact us and we will help with your request.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, this policy will be updated and the date above will change. Significant changes will be shown on this page before they take effect.
